# WNBA Game Rules

Each player owns **2 WNBA teams**. Points below accrue per owned team and
sum to that player's total WNBA score for the season.

| Event                     | Points |
|-----------------------------|--------|
| Regular-season win           | +6     |
| Regular-season loss          | -2     |
| Qualify for playoffs         | +10    |
| Win Round 1                  | +15    |
| Win Semifinals                | +25    |
| Win WNBA Championship          | +35    |

Mimics NBA's scoring style, scaled for a shorter, conference-less season.
The WNBA plays a 44-game regular season (about half of NBA's 82), so
win/loss points are doubled — 6/-2 here versus NBA's 3/-1, the same 3:1
ratio at twice the scale. The WNBA has also used a single unified
standings table since 2022 (no conferences), with an 8-team bracket
seeded 1v8/2v7/3v6/4v5: Round 1 (best-of-3), Semifinals (best-of-5), and
the Finals (best-of-7 as of 2025). That's 3 playoff rounds, not NBA's 4 —
there's no conference-championship stage to score separately.

Roster: 2 teams per player, drawn from the current 15-team league (see
`CLAUDE.md` for the roster, including the Portland Fire/Toronto Tempo
2026 expansion notes). With 6 players, max demand is 12 of 15 teams —
comfortable surplus.
