# ATP (Men's Tennis) Game Rules

Each player owns **2 countries**. Unlike every other league, the "team" you
draft is a country, not a club — a country's score at a major is the **sum
across every one of that country's players** in the singles draw, tallying
how many of them won each round. Majors only (no regular-season tour
events).

| Round win                        | Points |
|--------------------------------------|--------|
| 1st round                              | +2     |
| 2nd round                               | +3     |
| 3rd round                                | +5     |
| 4th round                                 | +5     |
| 5th round (quarterfinal)                   | +7     |
| 6th round (semifinal)                       | +8     |
| Final                                        | +10    |

Round wins stack cumulatively per player — a player who wins the title
banks 2+3+5+5+7+8+10 = **40 points** for their country. Across all 4 majors
in a season, a country that produced the champion every single time would
max out at 40 × 4 = **160 points**. A country with multiple players in the
draw earns points for each of them independently — e.g. if Country X has
two players who both win their 1st round match, that's +2 twice (+4 total),
even if one of them then loses in the 2nd round while the other keeps
advancing.
