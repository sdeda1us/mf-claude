# MLS Game Rules

Each player owns **4 MLS teams**. Points below accrue per owned team and
sum to that player's total MLS score for the season.

| Event                                          | Points |
|-------------------------------------------------|--------|
| Per point in the standings                        | +2     |
| Per goal of goal differential (GD)                  | +1     |
| Win the Supporters' Shield (best overall record)      | +15    |
| Qualify for the playoffs                                | +10    |
| Win Round One (reach Conference Semifinals)               | +10    |
| Win Conference Semifinal (reach Conference Final)            | +15    |
| Win Conference Final (reach MLS Cup)                            | +25    |
| Win MLS Cup                                                       | +30    |

The regular-season base is identical in style and weight to EPL's (points
+ goal differential) — MLS's season length is close enough to EPL's that
no rescaling was needed. On top of that, MLS has an actual playoff bracket
(EPL doesn't), so cumulative round bonuses are layered on in this app's
usual style, at the same weights as NBA/NHL's (10/10/15/25/30). The
Supporters' Shield — the single best regular-season record across all 30
teams, no conference split — gets its own bonus on top of whatever a team
earns from the table itself.

Bonuses stack cumulatively: MLS Cup winner Inter Miami CF banks the
playoff-qualification, Round One, Conference Semifinal, and Conference
Final bonuses too, not just the +30 for the Cup itself.

Roster: 4 teams per player, drawn from the full 30-team league (see
`CLAUDE.md` for the roster and the 2027 calendar-change notes).
