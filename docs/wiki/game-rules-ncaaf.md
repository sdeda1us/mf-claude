# NCAAF (College Football) Game Rules

Each player owns **5 NCAAF teams**, with **at most 1** from a "minor"
conference. Points below accrue per owned team and sum to that player's
total NCAAF score for the season.

**Major conferences:** SEC, Big Ten, ACC, Big 12, and Notre Dame (independent,
but treated as major given its historical power-conference scheduling).
**Minor conferences:** American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Sun
Belt, Pac-12, and all other independents (currently UConn). At least 4 of a
player's 5 teams must come from the major group.

| Event                                   | Points |
|--------------------------------------------|--------|
| Win (regular season or bowl/playoff game)   | +12    |
| Loss (regular season)                        | -6     |
| Loss (bowl or playoff game)                   | 0      |
| Playoff bid (make the CFP bracket)             | +10    |
| Playoff win (any round before the title game) or bye into next round | +10 |
| CFP Championship Game bid                        | +20    |
| CFP Championship Game winner                      | +30    |

Unlike the other leagues' rules, wins/losses here include postseason games —
a bowl or playoff loss just scores 0 instead of the usual -6, rather than
being excluded from the win/loss tally entirely. "BCS bowl" in the original
rules (a system retired in 2014) maps to the current 12-team College
Football Playoff: a "bowl bid" is making the CFP bracket at all, a "bowl
win" is any playoff win short of the title game. The top 4 seeds get a
first-round bye straight to the quarterfinal — that bye scores the same
+10 as an actual playoff win, the same way NFL's first-round bye does (see
[game-rules-nfl.md](game-rules-nfl.md)).
