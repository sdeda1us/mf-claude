# F1 (Formula 1) Game Rules

Each player owns **1 F1 constructor** (a works team fielding two cars all
season — think of it as a club, like an EPL team, not an individual
driver). All stats below combine both cars' results across the season.

| Event                                          | Points |
|-------------------------------------------------|--------|
| Per Constructors' Championship point              | +0.1   |
| Constructors' Championship — 1st                    | +10    |
| Constructors' Championship — 2nd                      | +5     |

A constructor's score is 0.1× its actual real-world Constructors'
Championship points total for the season (an exact, official number — e.g.
a 450-point season is worth 45), plus a flat bonus for finishing 1st or 2nd
in the final standings. The bonus is exclusive across places — nothing for
3rd or lower, and the champion doesn't also collect the 2nd-place bonus.

Roster: 1 team per player, drawn from the current 11-constructor grid (see
`CLAUDE.md` for the roster and season dates, including the Cadillac/Audi
notes). With 6 players and an 11-team pool, at least 5 constructors always
go undrafted — comfortable surplus, no risk of the pool running dry.
