# WTA (Women's Tennis) Game Rules

Identical rules and roster spec to [ATP](game-rules-tennis-mens.md) — each
player owns **2 countries**, and a country's score at a major sums across
every one of that country's players in the draw. Majors only.

| Round win                        | Points |
|--------------------------------------|--------|
| 1st round                              | +2     |
| 2nd round                               | +3     |
| 3rd round                                | +5     |
| 4th round                                 | +5     |
| 5th round (quarterfinal)                   | +7     |
| 6th round (semifinal)                       | +8     |
| Final                                        | +10    |

A champion's country banks 40 points for that major (2+3+5+5+7+8+10); a
country producing the champion at all 4 majors in a season would max out
at 160.
