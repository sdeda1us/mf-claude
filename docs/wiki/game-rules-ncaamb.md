# NCAAMB (Men's College Basketball) Game Rules

Each player owns **7 NCAAMB teams**, with **at most 2** from a "minor"
conference. Points below accrue per owned team and sum to that player's
total NCAAMB score for the season.

**Major conferences:** SEC, Big Ten, ACC, Big 12, Big East.
**Minor conferences:** every other Division I conference (America East,
American, ASUN, Atlantic 10, Big Sky, Big South, Big West, CAA, Conference
USA, Horizon, Ivy, Metro/MAAC, MAC, MEAC, Missouri Valley, Mountain West,
NEC, OVC, Pac-12, Patriot, SoCon, Southland, SWAC, Summit, Sun Belt, United
Athletic, WCC). At least 5 of a player's 7 teams must come from the major
group.

| Event                                                | Points |
|----------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| Win (regular season or conference tournament)              | +3     |
| Loss                                                        | -1     |
| Regular-season conference champion (#1 seed in conf. tourney) | +5   |
| Conference tournament champion                                | +10  |
| NCAA Tournament qualifier                                     | +15  |
| — additional bonus if a #1 seed                                | +10  |
| — additional bonus if a #2 seed                                 | +5   |
| Win Round of 64 (1st round)                                      | +5   |
| Win Round of 32 (2nd round)                                       | +5   |
| Win Sweet 16 (3rd round)                                           | +5   |
| Win Elite Eight (4th round)                                         | +10  |
| Win Final Four (5th round)                                           | +10  |
| Win National Championship                                             | +25  |

The #1/#2 seed bonuses stack on top of the flat +15 NCAA Tournament
qualifying bonus (e.g. a #1 overall seed banks 15 + 10 = 25 points just for
making the field, before any tournament wins).
